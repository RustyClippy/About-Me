About Me

Always full of suggestions, just like the legendary paperclip.

My journey started with one simple question: how do the apps I use every day actually work? That curiosity turned into a habit of building things, breaking them, and rebuilding them better.

I started with the foundations of the web — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — and I'm actively expanding into Python, AI, and Machine Learning. Every repo on my profile is a checkpoint, not a museum. I learn in public, mistakes included.

My approach is product-first: before writing any code, I ask does this actually solve a real problem for a real user? I study architecture and best practices early, so I scale on strong foundations instead of technical debt.
